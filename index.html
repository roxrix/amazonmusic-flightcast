01 · The exchange

What they want back

Bring your audience, then bring the campaign to life.

  • Website: a button or link on your site pointing to your show on Amazon Music
  • Audio: an ad at the start of your episodes, played 750,000 times, naming Amazon Music and no other platform
  • Social: one Instagram feed post tagging @amazonmusic using the photos from the shoot. The account posting it needs at least 250,000 followers
  • Display: a banner campaign seen at least 100,000 times, linking to your show on Amazon Music
  • Newsletter: an email to at least 100,000 people, pointing them to Amazon Music and no other platform

Assets to send

  • A video of you, 15 to 30 seconds, talking about the show
  • A headshot you are happy with, which they use to build a still image ad
  • Cover art at 3000 × 3000 pixels, JPG, or MOV if you want it to loop
  • A tagline, 40 characters or less

02 · Quickest

About 3 weeks from sending it over to going live.

Being featured in the Amazon Music app

It reaches people already looking for something to listen to.

What you get

Amazon puts your show in front of people browsing the app. That might be a spot at the top of a page, or a place on one of their themed lists. How prominent it is comes down to Amazon's team, who look at how big your audience is, how good the show is, and how well it fits what they are pushing at the time.

What they want back: Nothing. You are not promising them anything for this one.

03 · No money changes hands

Ad space for ad space

Swap ad space with Amazon Music

You trade ad space you already have for ad space you could not buy.

What you get

Ads for your show play to people using the free version of Amazon Music. That is their whole free audience, including plenty of people who have never come across your show.

↔

What they want back

An ad telling your own listeners that the show is on Amazon Music, played 2 million times across your episodes.

  1. You record an audio ad pointing your listeners to your show on Amazon Music
  2. You run it in your own episodes, the same way you would run any other ad, until it has been played 2 million times
  3. Amazon runs ads for your show to people on the free tier of Amazon Music
  4. Nobody pays anybody. It is ad space for ad space

How long the 2 million takes is down to your download numbers. A show doing 100,000 downloads an episode gets there faster than one doing 10,000, so it is worth working out your own timeline before committing.

Ads on social, paid for by Amazon

Someone else pays, and clips you already posted can do the work.

What you get

Amazon pays for ads promoting your show on social media. It does not cost you anything. There are three things you can give them, and you can do more than one.

The social ads, in detail

TikTok · Instagram · Facebook

Send the files. Amazon funds the reach.

You send Amazon the videos and photos, they pay to run them as ads on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. The ads go out under your name and Amazon's at the same time, so they reach more people but still look like they came from you, and they do not clutter up your own feed. Every ad links back to your show.

Send all three kinds if you can. You need a professional or business Instagram account to take part on Instagram and Facebook. Any TikTok account works.

Filming it

  • Plain background, camera on something steady. Not handheld, not a selfie
  • Having your mic and headphones in shot works well

The file

  • 15 to 30 seconds long, under 500 MB
  • Upright, wide or square all work
  • Add subtitles if you can. Amazon adds the closing frame

What to say

  • Say who you are, then what the show is: the sort of conversations you have, a story that would make someone stop and listen
  • Keep it general so it does not date. No naming particular episodes or guests
  • Finish with "Listen to the podcast now on Amazon Music", or "Watch the podcast now on Amazon Music" if you make video

The still image ad

One good, high-resolution headshot. Amazon builds the ad around it and links it to your show.

Clips from the show

  • 30 seconds or less, taken from video episodes that are already on Amazon Music
  • Clips you have already posted are fine
