01 · What you do

Five requirements

Send your audience to Amazon Music.

Amazon asks you to do all five.

Website

Add a link to your show on Amazon Music.

750,000 plays

Run a pre-roll naming Amazon Music and no other platform until it plays 750,000 times.

250,000 followers

Post one shoot photo on Instagram and tag @amazonmusic. The account needs at least 250,000 followers.

100,000 impressions

Run a display banner linking to Amazon Music until it reaches 100,000 impressions.

100,000 people

Email at least 100,000 people and link only to Amazon Music.
